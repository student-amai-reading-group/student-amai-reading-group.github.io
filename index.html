Student Applied Math & AI Reading Group

A reading group run by students at ETH Zürich interested in research, covering papers in applied mathematics and AI. We usually meet on Sunday or Monday evening, and afterwards we sometimes play social deduction or card games.

How the papers connect

Solid arrows are direct citations, pointing from a paper to the work it cites. Dashed lines join papers that lean on the same references without citing each other. Hollow circles were referenced in a session rather than presented. Entries with no arXiv link — a thesis, a write-up — are left out.
